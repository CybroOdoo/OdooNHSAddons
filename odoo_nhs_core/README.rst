NHS Backoffice Management Core
==============================
One-click installer for the complete NHS Backoffice suite. Installing this
module installs all 13 NHS modules and adds a single administrator role that
grants full management access across the whole suite.

Included Modules
================
Trust & Organisation
--------------------
* ``odoo_nhs_trust_management`` - NHS Trust Management
* ``odoo_nhs_trust_operations`` - NHS Trust - Operations & Compliance
* ``odoo_nhs_trust_reports`` - NHS Trust - Reports & Documents
* ``odoo_nhs_ods_sync`` - NHS Trust Management - ODS Sync
* ``odoo_nhs_uk_regions`` - NHS Trust - UK Regions Extension

Governance & Clinical Quality
-----------------------------
* ``odoo_nhs_governance`` - NHS Governance Management
* ``odoo_nhs_incident_risk`` - NHS Incident & Risk Management
* ``odoo_nhs_complaints`` - NHS Complaints & PALS Management
* ``odoo_nhs_dspt`` - NHS DSPT Compliance

Workforce
---------
* ``odoo_nhs_establishment`` - NHS Establishment Register
* ``odoo_nhs_recruitment`` - NHS Recruitment Pipeline
* ``odoo_nhs_training`` - NHS Mandatory Training Register

Estates & Facilities
--------------------
* ``odoo_nhs_estate`` - NHS Estate Register

Security
========
**NHS Backoffice Administrator** - an umbrella group that implies the highest
access level of every NHS module (Trust Administrator, ODS Sync Operator,
Governance Manager, Incident Quality Lead and Safeguarding, Complaints Quality
Lead, DSPT Manager, Workforce Manager, Recruitment Manager, Training Manager and
Estate Manager). Lower roles are still assigned per module as usual.
